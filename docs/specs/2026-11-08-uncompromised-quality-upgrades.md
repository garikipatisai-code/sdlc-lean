## Goal

Deliver 5 quality tracks sequentially with dual critics. No track completes without verify green and both critics passing.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Q-1: All 5 tracks are delivered; fewer than 5 fails.
- Q-2: Tracks complete in the planned sequential order.
- Q-3: Dual independent critics review each track and pass; unreviewed or failing review fails.
- Q-4: `npm run verify` passes on the delivered state.

## Out-of-scope
