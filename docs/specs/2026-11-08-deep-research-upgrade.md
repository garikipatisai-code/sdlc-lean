## Goal

Upgrade deep research with diversity, freshness, and dedup plus explicit contradiction and uncertainty handling scored by deterministic metrics.

## Acceptance Criteria

- D-1: Results cover diverse sources; single-source answers fail.
- D-2: Stale sources are excluded or flagged by a binary freshness check.
- D-3: Duplicates are merged or removed; duplicate output fails.
- D-4: Conflicting evidence and uncertain claims are explicitly surfaced.
- D-5: Quality is scored by deterministic metrics that repeat identically on rerun.

## Out-of-scope

Model or provider changes, unrelated skills, and any approval action.
